# RetainerProof validation playbook

## 14-day gate

The build moves to a limited beta only after:

- 30 personal outreach messages sent
- 10 interviews completed
- 3 refundable $5 founding reservations paid

If fewer than three people reserve, change the audience or promise and repeat the test. Do not use more features as the default answer.

## Interview script

1. How many websites do you maintain on a recurring plan?
2. What do you send clients at the end of the month today?
3. How long does one report take?
4. Which tools or documents are involved?
5. When do you skip the report, and why?
6. Has a client ever asked what their maintenance fee pays for?
7. Which parts of maintenance would you never want shown in a report?
8. Would a ten-minute, evidence-based monthly report be worth $5 per month for three clients?

Do not pitch until question six is complete. Capture exact phrases, current behavior, time, and the next agreed action.

## Personal outreach

Subject: A clearer monthly story for website care clients

> Hi [name] — I’m building a small tool for independent web care professionals. It records maintenance in seconds, watches public client sites, and creates a report clients can actually understand. I’m looking for ten honest 20-minute conversations, not a sales call. Would you be open to showing me how you report maintenance today?

Reservation follow-up:

> Thanks again. The founding reservation is $5, refundable before launch, and becomes your first Starter month. Founding users keep the $5 Starter price. Here is the private reservation page: [URL]

## Evidence sheet

Record: contact, niche, client count, report method, minutes per report, skipped-report reason, value-question history, quote, reservation status, next step, and follow-up date.

## Beta thresholds

- At least 70% create their first report in ten minutes
- At least 60% send another report the following month
- No serious workspace-isolation, billing, or delivery incident
